About Us

"« The Authenticity of the Present and the Nobility of the Past » The name of the restaurant was derived from its position in the ancient Assyrian settlement of Mailthaya in the Malta region. The restaurant, which serves traditional Kurdish and Iraqi fare in its main hall and gardens that are home to long-lived Perennial pine and ginnar trees, was established inside Malta's largest and oldest park in the city of Duhok in 2007 and quickly became a well-known landmark of the city. On May 5, 2022, the restaurant was reorganized and opened under a new professional administration to transform the restaurant"
